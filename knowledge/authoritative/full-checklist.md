## C) Venue Fit Qualification
- Hard NO conditions:
- ceiling height under 6m
- insufficient ground space for inflatable
- ground not flat or clear of sharp objects or hazards
- ceiling height under 6m (Monster), under 9m (Mega Bounce House)
- Hard NO details: Minimum 6m ceiling clearance for Monster and 9m for Mega Bounce House and enough ground space to fit the inflatable are required. The ground must be flat and clear of sharp objects or anything that could damage the inflatable. As long as the inflatable can be staked or bolted, most surfaces are suitable.

## E) Production / Technical Rider
- Power plan: All power contingencies, including provision of backup generator, are promoter's responsibility and risk. We recommend a backup generator if main power is from a generator.
- Lighting / sound / staging: Always up to promoter unless we are handling full event production.
- Bar & food: All catering and bar setups outside the UK are strictly the promoter's responsibility; no involvement from us. For UK gigs, we only provide bar/food if we're running the setup ourselves—otherwise, promoter handles everything including licensing.
- Queueing & crowd flow: Groups of 4–6 are admitted to the Monster at a time, adjusted based on inflatable size and group numbers. A staff member at the entrance gives a safety talk and warm-up to each group before entry. As a group passes a certain section, the next group is allowed through, creating a flow of approximately one group every 30 seconds. Coordination is managed by the staff at the front and on the Monster, using cues to control entry.
- On-site comms: One radio with earpiece is required for each team member and all on-site staff, including medics and security. radios, All radios must have an earpiece. in-ear
